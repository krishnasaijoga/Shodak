Can AI Prevent Sexual Violence? A Future We Must Build

It was 10 PM when Pranavi walked down a quiet lane from the Delhi Metro to her PG. She didn’t notice a man following her from the metro, but the AI-powered CCTV did.

The system detected unusual behavior: a lone woman walking, a person tailing her, and increasing tension. It immediately flagged the situation and alerted the nearby beat cop.

As he tried to grab her hand, a voice was heard from the nearby camera saying, “You are being watched; the police are on the way.”

The man froze. Pranavi escaped, and the man was caught within an hour by the police. The following week, Pranavi gave her statement: “I didn’t know the cameras were watching. I’m glad those cameras didn’t blink.”

This is a glimpse into how AI is reshaping public safety — especially in preventing sexual violence.

So, how can AI shift the needle on the prevention of sexual violence? Let us break it down.

AI in the Prevention of Sexual Violence

Predictive Safety Measures
AI-driven platforms like the Safecity app detect heatmaps of harassment and hotspots. It helps authorities deploy preventive measures at such places and times where such instances are more likely to occur.
Real-Time Surveillance
Deep Vision of Europe is developing AI-powered systems that monitor public places to detect patterns like groping, stalking, or harassment in real time.
Personal Safety Apps
Apps like the Circle of 6 detect anomalies and alert the user, nearby contacts, or authorities if they enter high-risk areas.
Dark Web Monitoring
Dark web hosting forums and websites that facilitate sexual crimes are detected by law enforcement agencies worldwide. AI can be used to scan these websites and prevent them.
Educational Platforms
AI platforms like Rewire can help guide them through empathetic exercises and real-time roleplays to reframe their views on consent, power dynamics, and sexual boundaries, reducing the likelihood of impulsive aggression.
Research and Policy
AI can be used in streamlining processes and provide valuable insights for informed policy-making decisions. However, it cannot replace human insights in policy-making.
But with great power comes great responsibility. AI is not devoid of limitations.

Limitations of AI

Ethical Concerns
Use of AI in surveillance is like a sword of Damocles. It can infringe on civil liberties and privacy rights. It can be misused for profiling, discrimination, or worse. So, it is important that we create mechanisms that do not allow its misuse.
Abuse in Creating Harmful Content
Expansion of AI usage is not under control, so is its misuse. There are 3500 AI-generated child sexual abuse images uploaded to the dark web. This calls for urgent policy action and the development of technological countermeasures to combat it.
Conclusion

AI is a powerful tool in the prevention of sexual violence. It can be used for the detection of incidents, real-time surveillance, personal safety, dark web monitoring, educating young adults, and policy research.

AI alone can’t end the sexual violence issue. But with ethical oversight and human empathy, it can become a silent guardian — a watchful protector, a dark knight guiding us to a safer society.
