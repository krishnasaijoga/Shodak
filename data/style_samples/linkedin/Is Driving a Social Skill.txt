Is Driving a Social Skill?
We often think driving as a technical skill--steering, accelerating, and braking. But, what if we rethink it as a social skill in modern times?
From giving way to ambulances to signaling with empathy in rush hour traffic, driving is full of human interactions that mirror teamwork, patience, conflict resolution, and shared responsibility.

What is a Social Skill?

effectively with others. It helps us form deeper,
more meaningful connections.
